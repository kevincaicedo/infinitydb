Claims & evidence

Every number or comparative statement in InfinityDB’s public copy must have an Allowed row in the project’s claim ledger, citing a validated artifact (replicated runs, clean tree, recorded environment, competitors in the same run). A claim absent from the ledger does not exist — and this website is checked against the ledger in CI.

The ledger lives in the repository at docs/claim-ledger.md (a snapshot used by the site’s CI check is committed as _ledger-snapshot.md). The full methodology — harnesses, environment rules, replicate discipline, the instrument checks — and every currently-Allowed number with its artifact live on the benchmarks & evidence page; the binding comparative campaign report is published verbatim as an evidence page. Pipelined peaks, always-mode write rates, absolute tail-latency claims, and every tiered-storage number are still Narrowed or Evidence-pending, so they are absent by rule, not by oversight.

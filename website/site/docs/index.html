Claims & evidence

Every number or comparative statement in InfinityDB’s public copy must have an Allowed row in the project’s claim ledger, citing a validated artifact (replicated runs, clean tree, recorded environment, competitors in the same run). A claim absent from the ledger does not exist — and this website is checked against the ledger in CI.

The ledger lives in the repository at docs/claim-ledger.md (a snapshot used by the site’s CI check is committed as _ledger-snapshot.md). The benchmark story, honestly: performance and comparison numbers for the alpha are still moving through the evidence pipeline, so this site publishes correctness evidence (simulation sweeps, crash matrices, byte-compat counts) and leaves throughput/latency marketing at zero until the ledger allows otherwise.
